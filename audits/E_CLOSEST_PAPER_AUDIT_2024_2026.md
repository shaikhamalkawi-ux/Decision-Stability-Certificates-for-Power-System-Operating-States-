# Workstream E - closest-paper audit, 2024-2026

## Search conclusion

The audit found close work in Wasserstein distributionally robust dispatch and
unit commitment, AC-feasibility restoration, and feasible-path certification.
It did not identify a paper whose primary object is the same post hoc,
support-explicit admission diagnostic for empirical operating laws across a
static/conditional/chronological/AC information ladder. This is a bounded
closest-paper audit, not a proof of absence.

| Work | What it does | Boundary relative to DSC-Grid |
|---|---|---|
| A two-step Wasserstein distributionally robust chance- and security-constrained dispatch, TPWRS 2024, DOI 10.1109/TPWRS.2023.3242468 | Optimizes dispatch against a Wasserstein ambiguity set and chance/security constraints. | Robust prescriptive optimization; not post hoc transport of an empirical operating law to declared supports. |
| Multi-stage fully adaptive distributionally robust unit commitment, Applied Energy 2024, DOI 10.1016/j.apenergy.2024.124051 | Uses Wasserstein uncertainty sets in multi-stage UC. | Chronology is inside a robust decision model; it does not compare static admission, mean repair, and separate AC projection for one fixed target law. |
| Distributionally robust bilevel optimization for distribution networks, TSTE 2025, DOI 10.1109/TSTE.2024.3509314 | Wasserstein bilevel planning/operation with demand response. | Different decision and uncertainty object; no empirical admission certificate. |
| Two-stage distributionally robust dispatch with small-sample wind/PV data, TSG 2026, DOI 10.1109/TSG.2025.3621481 | Constructs a Wasserstein DRO dispatch after data augmentation. | Prescriptive small-sample robustness, not support-sensitive law diagnostics. |
| Conditional DRO for look-ahead dispatch, TPWRS early access 2026, DOI 10.1109/TPWRS.2026.3697741 | Models heterogeneous uncertainties with conditional ambiguity information. | Conditional robust optimization; does not study the same empirical-law admission hierarchy. |
| Towards AC feasibility of DCOPF dispatch, accepted PSCC 2026 / EPSR article 113652, DOI 10.1016/j.epsr.2026.113652 | Recovers AC-feasible points from DCOPF dispatch using structured ACPF. | Closest AC-restoration neighbor; DSC-Grid uses a direct nonlinear L1 projection as a separate witness layer and makes no pipeline superiority claim. |
| Certifying the nonexistence of feasible paths between operating points, IEEE CSL 2025, DOI 10.1109/LCSYS.2025.3644800 | Certifies disconnectedness / absence of a continuous feasible path. | Topological path question, not distributional target admission or mean repair. |

## Claim retained

The defensible novelty claim is methodological and narrow: DSC-Grid separates
mean movement, target-law shape, conditional support, chronological mean
admission/repair, and AC restoration, with exact/bound/witness labels and an
operational-data instantiation. It does not claim priority for Wasserstein
DRO, weak transport in general, unit-commitment modelling, feasible paths, or
DC-to-AC recovery.
