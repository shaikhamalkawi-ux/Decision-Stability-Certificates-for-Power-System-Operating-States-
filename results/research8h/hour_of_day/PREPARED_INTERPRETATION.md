# Clarifications after the preparation freeze, before any solve

The producer and protocol are unchanged from the preparation freeze (source SHA256 `b617d41ab09d18d57c003147b52ae80f369225bc6029121109cee3d53e71d60e`, protocol SHA256 `09b3a45af4224c65b03dd4d3972750ba417f0f26ccc153e51fa9ca3ae4d9829b`). These clarifications address two reporting ambiguities identified in independent review. They change no permutation, model, bound, source, routing, witness or solver option and require no repeat preparation.

The original `changed_adjacent_pair_count` and `changed_adjacent_pairs_after_hours` fields count **broken source continuity**: for destination transition t to t+1, the criterion is `order[t+1] != order[t] + 1`. This is not the count of all positionwise changed source pairs. For example, moving two consecutive source hours together changes the pair's position but retains source continuity. The separate `adjacency_addendum.json` records both definitions for all 167 transitions, with lists of affected destination transitions and their source pairs. Its literal positionwise criterion is `(order[t], order[t+1]) != (t, t+1)`. Neither measure is a claim of meteorological realism. The source maps, complete-package preservation and hour-of-day invariants were already fixed and remain authoritative.

The protocol's “at most 1200 seconds” describes the configured solve/check allocation and admission guard, **not a guaranteed process wall-time cap**. The frozen runner starts its allocation clock after entry hash validation and native-model loading; those operations and preparation are excluded. It stops initiating an LP with fewer than 30 allocation seconds remaining, or a MIP with fewer than 305 seconds remaining. It does not forcibly interrupt a solver or exact verification at 1200 seconds. Solvers can overrun their requested time limits and post-solve exact checks can exceed the remaining allocation. All actual timings and any overrun must be reported without calling the budget a hard deadline or a performance benchmark.

Any later execution requires independent prepared PASS and explicit root GO. It must receive a separate execution/completion timing audit recording the observed process start/end, the allocation start if instrumented without changing frozen code, reported phase elapsed time, configured 1200 seconds and any positive overrun. A timestamp inferred from a file modification time must be labeled approximate rather than presented as an observed exact allocation start. This sidecar and the arithmetic-only adjacency addendum sit outside the original 125-file manifest and are separately reviewable; the original manifest hash remains unchanged.
