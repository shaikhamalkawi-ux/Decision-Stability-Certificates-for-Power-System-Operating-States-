## Mechanism and scope

The primary-only first-week213/231collisions lose a distinguishing cluster-label chronology; their full Hindex differs. The three stronger collisions instead retain that chronology and still identify different raw sequences. For those cases the information loss is consistent with replacement of distinct days assigned to the same representative-day class. It is not evidence that transition counts omit an otherwise retained distinction in the cluster-label sequence, because the full sequence matches too.

These findings characterize a declared preprocessing observation, not a full operational implementation or theoretical minimal-information bound. Auer's method deliberately compresses original profiles; its reference construction and operational assumptions must be respected in any claimed comparison. Priority and publication significance require more than observing a non-injective compression. No performance advantage or independent-network transfer is claimed.
