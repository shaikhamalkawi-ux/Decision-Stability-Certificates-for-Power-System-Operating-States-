The PDF stays in the private reading workspace and is excluded from deliveries.

Section 3, especially equations 8–10 on page 7, derives rigorous objective lower bounds using an approximate multiplier, its stationarity residual and finite variable boxes. Page8 requires applying the bound to the raw model when presolve transformations lack rigorous justification. Page9 distinguishes rigorous upper witnesses from numerical points. Section 4, equations 13–14 on page 9, verifies infeasibility by enclosing a ray residual over the box; page 10 expressly treats a nonpositive bound as inconclusive.

For our declared expanded model, inserting expanded row endpoints and boxes into this established framework gives our signed endpoint formulas. The explicit tau norm terms are algebraic consequences. Exact binary64-as-rational arithmetic and the archived checker are implementation choices, not a new general certification theorem. Our next manuscript version should cite this body-level precedent while retaining its own complete derivation and strict-versus-expanded distinction.
