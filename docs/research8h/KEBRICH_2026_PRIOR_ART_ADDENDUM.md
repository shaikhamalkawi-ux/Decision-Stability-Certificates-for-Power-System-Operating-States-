Heinrichs, *Robust capacity expansion modeling for renewable energy systems*, iScience 29(3), 114929 (2026), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isci.2026.114929); [open article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12996788/). Read the algorithm, results, limitations, reference linear program, and modification descriptions. The authors test fixed investment designs across 40 weather years, identify supply gaps, and modify planning through demand changes, synthetic time series or added inequalities. Their reference dispatch model is continuous despite the operational label UC; that label alone does not establish thermal commitment binaries. Limitations include one demand series, historical weather, an isolated Germany model and perfect foresight.

Relevance is an inference: operational validation followed by information-directed refinement is established prior work and cannot be our broad novelty. Our clock-conditioned paired observations and exact certificates address a different, narrower question. No method comparison, exact-rational audit of their code, or priority proof was performed.
