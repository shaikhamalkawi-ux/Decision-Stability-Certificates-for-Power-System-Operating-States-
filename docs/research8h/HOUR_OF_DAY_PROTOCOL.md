It is a separate sensitivity experiment on the same RTS Area 1 January week, not a blind replication, independent network, or novelty claim. Earlier arms remain immutable. Preparation, independent review and explicit root GO precede every optimizer call.

Use the archived January identity full-DC/native-UC model: 168 hours, 41 dispatch units, 24 commitment units, 24 buses, 38 lines, no shedding, no named-unit means, and the unchanged 23195 MWh cap on the same 23 Coal/Oil/NG units. There are exactly two ordinary seeds, 26093200 and 26093201, in that order. For each seed create one `numpy.random.Generator(numpy.random.PCG64(seed))`. Start with `order=arange(168)`. Never redraw an identity or duplicate sample. Keep both cases in the denominator even if their permutations or outcomes coincide.

Sort keys lexicographically (hour first, then every U bit); within a key, retain increasing hour order. Recompute canonical startup/shutdown variables from that sequence; do not permute transition indicators as if they were exogenous data.

Report both ordinary cases, with exact expanded positives, exact robust negatives and UNKNOWN separately, and report the identity/control checks outside that denominator.
