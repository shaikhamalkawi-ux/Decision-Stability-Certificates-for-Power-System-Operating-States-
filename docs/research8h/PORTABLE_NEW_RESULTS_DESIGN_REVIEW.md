# Independent design review: portable new closed results

Verdict: the closed design PORTABLE_NEW_RESULTS_REPLAY_DESIGN.md is mathematically coherent and appropriately bounded. This review authorizes no candidate preparation, replay, installation, optimizer call or change to producer files. Implementation/protocol and prepared-package gates remain separate.

The proposed denominator is correct: six uncapped energy models with six exact objective lower bounds, two original-reference binary uppers and four target binary uppers, four exact interval records, four continuous restricted-model points, and two auxiliary full HOD binary control points giving four inherited memberships. The last two categories are different kinds of evidence and must remain separate. A zero-mask point check cannot become original-mask binary acceptance, even if a generic checker field is vacuously true under that zero mask.

The objective-bound formula correctly retains the finite-box stationarity residual and its expansion penalty. Exact zero-dual baselines may be negative after bound widening and must not be clipped. Explicit raw-to-projected dual admissibility, every nonzero rational residual coordinate, direct widened-endpoint comparison, full original-mask witness membership and unchanged P/theta recovery are material. Interval algebra must retain both identity lower and upper bounds; an incumbent comparison is not the optimal-to-optimal interval. Capped seed26093211 remains UNKNOWN because its new lower bound is below its cap plus tau.

Model inheritance must be independently checked rather than inferred from labels or shared matrix hashes. Identities retain an identity parent-row map to their already uncapped reference; targets retain the row selection deleting the capped parent's sole cap row. Both relationships must be checked against their respective parents. The energy objective must agree with native fuel roster and actual deleted cap support. The locality48 rule depends on every actual state-coordinate support hour and must retain all transition and exclusivity rows. Auxiliary controls require their own full boxes, masks and vectors.

The provenance plan correctly distinguishes fresh producer manifests relative to the arm directory from subset manifests relative to the package root, historical absolute manifests under exactly two explicit prefix maps, and a complete outer package manifest. Normalized-path collisions, prefix lookalikes, traversal, unmapped paths, conflicting hashes and missing outer coverage must fail. Every listed historical binding is checked as written; a historical 61-file output dictionary is not a claim that a later documented package contains only those 61 files. Native source payloads are preserved provenance, not a claim that their model assembler was re-executed.

The old wrapper and the new wrapper cover a declared union of selected results. They do not numerically replay the two unrestricted first-week optimal-energy intervals; those retain separate independent evidence. Therefore neither 'all figure rows' nor 'every historical experiment' is an appropriate description. Same-host relocation also remains distinct from second-machine portability or complete raw-data model reconstruction.

The planned synthetic checks target consequential routing/mathematical failures rather than repeat solver output. Important final code-gate items are complete full-mask and zero-mask distinctions, rational/display rounding in both directions, absent-upper rejection, week/reference matching, original-row provenance, manifest-relative bases and final package rehash/set closure. Reports must be new external files and cannot mutate the package. A failure must preserve partial reports without writing the all-cases PASS or automatically retrying.
