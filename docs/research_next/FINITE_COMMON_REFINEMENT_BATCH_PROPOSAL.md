One batch, at most8 new master nominations and2400 seconds overall. If any arm lacks its required valid point/cut, stop the batch immediately with UNKNOWN. An exact candidate-state hash prevents repeated proposals; a repeat stops rather than triggering another solve. A previous cut must reject its originating nominee exactly.

A common positive settles only this fixed pair/cap/model and information timing. A backend master infeasible/timeout or exhausted8 rounds does not prove common binary infeasibility. A positive does not prove small regret; a negative search outcome does not prove information loss.

## Sound numerical transport of new exact cuts

For an exact necessary state row a*z>=b, let s>0 be a single declared dyadic scale (selected deterministically from max nonzero absolute coefficient, with its exact exponent archived). Write alpha=a/s and beta=b/s. Round each alpha_j once to binary64 bar_alpha_j, retaining every exact residual e_j=bar_alpha_j-alpha_j, including coordinates encoded to zero. All admissible binary states lie in[0,1], so E=sum min(e_j,0) is a valid lower bound on e*z. Therefore every exact-cut-feasible state satisfies bar_alpha*z>=beta+E. Choose the encoded lower endpoint by rounding beta+E downward to a finite binary64 value (nearest conversion followed by one nextafter toward negative infinity only when needed), and archive its exact residual. This is an outward relaxation; approximate coefficient equality is not claimed.

This simple proof uses all12096 original state coordinates, no coefficient dropping threshold, no extra binary tau and no extra tolerance on the exact cut. Complete actual-backend coefficient and endpoint readback is required; any internal omission must be represented in the recorded bar_alpha/e before admission, or fail the transport. A cut whose admitted numerical transport does not exclude its own nominee is a nonprogressing null; it cannot be silently renormalized. Exact nominee checking continues to use the original a,b regardless of backend feasibility tolerances. Source/protocol must freeze one deterministic scaling recipe before evaluating the first scientific cut. This paragraph is a mathematical transport design, not evidence that the pending cut has useful scale.
