# One discriminator for the temporal mechanism

27 September 2026. Analytic design only. No new model, case generation, optimizer, scientific arithmetic or paper search. This note does not authorize execution or change any closed evidence.

**Current verdict:** the common-binary test does not by itself isolate chronology-specific information loss. Its positive result would refute a no-common-commitment claim for one pair at one cap. Even a certified negative, with both individual positives, would establish insufficient information for that declared decision but would not identify the missing information as chronological boundary information. The strongest actual observer collision preserves the entire cluster-label chronology Hindex; it loses distinctions between raw profiles assigned to the same representative.

## What the existing controls do and do not isolate

Week1 identity/day321 preserves the multiset of complete107-coordinate hours and complete24-hour joint days, clock alignment, static parameters and first/last48hours. The full authentic projected observation and full Hindex agree under one compatible RP relabeling. These facts rule out changed annual/weekly marginal content, changed day-profile inventory and omitted cluster-label sequence as explanations. They do not hold the raw profile at a particular hour fixed: different days within the same cluster exchange positions, changing pointwise representative-profile approximation error and its interaction with a preselected commitment.

The historical fixed-anchor rejection illustrates this gap directly: its23 supporting rows are one aggregate balance and22 thermal upper bounds at zero-based hour119. It demonstrates a one-hour capacity shortfall under that anchor, without a residence or ramp row. The subsequently verified on/on-ramp implication shows those supplementary ramp checks add no restriction beyond the output rows for these two frozen sequences. Neither finding removes minimum up/down effects, proves individual day321 feasibility, or rules out another common commitment.

There is a useful impossibility of interpretation here. If each raw day exactly equaled its representative on every consumed projected coordinate, the complete representative tables plus full Hindex would reconstruct the projected hourly sequence. Equal objects would then give equal reconstructed sequences. Thus a nontrivial collision with full Hindex cannot simultaneously remove all within-cluster projected-profile error. Differences in omitted native coordinates would instead be a projection limitation. We cannot demonstrate a pure missing-order effect under this strongest observer simply by declaring approximation error controlled away. This is a logical reconstruction observation, not a new theorem or a claim of lossless projection for all107 coordinates.

This experiment can be valid for our original48-hour, mature-initial/free-terminal model without being an end-to-end reproduction of Auer's operational method. The inspected author24-hour route clips dwell to24 before constructing its medoid-reconstructed reference and uses its own circular representative-period conventions. A fair claim about that published operational benchmark requires a separately declared common input/reference class, matching capacity, network, service/cost, boundary and dwell semantics, followed by verification of the actual decisions it produces. Disabling the clip without proving the method's multi-period formulation is also not a faithful reproduction. Do not clip our reference to make comparison convenient.

The current adapter supplies a declared supported signal projection, not operational equivalence for all native107 components. Any causal attribution must retain this qualification and establish that omitted varying inputs are not an alternative explanation before extending the result to the author's consumed physics. The ablation result would remain a statement about our model and chosen observation/action contract.

Therefore neither the proposed ablation nor renaming incompatibility as regret is methodological novelty. Its possible value is a narrowly certified mechanism result, including its negative outcome if a common binary action exists.
