# From equal temporal summaries to verifiable operating decisions

Working research note, 27 September 2026. This note closes the observed evidence through the first finite-batch attempt. It is not submission-ready and does not replace a frozen manuscript.

## Abstract

Equal temporal summaries do not, by themselves, establish unavoidable operating loss. We evaluate this distinction using a version-pinned representative-period preprocessing implementation, original chronological unit-commitment models, and independently checked exact certificates. All six initially targeted hour-of-day comparisons were distinguished by the actual preprocessing. A separate predeclared family of 15 nonidentity whole-day orders yielded five primary-observation collisions, including three with equal full chronological cluster-index output under a compatible common relabeling. Both members of the selected first-week pair have individually feasible expanded-model binary witnesses, while their two known commitments fail cross-transfer. Those failures do not imply that every common commitment fails. Separately, an actual-code audit of a synthetic single-bus UnitCommitment.jl case certifies a positive difference between separately informed optimal costs of the declared expanded native models, but not decision regret. The contribution supported by these results is an auditable, version-bound evaluation and a separation of proof claims. General algorithmic novelty, an information lower bound, and field validation are not established.

## 1. Question and relation to prior work

Representative-period aggregation and chronological boundary approximations address related but distinct sources of error. Auer et al. explicitly evaluate boundary reformulation against a chronological reference reconstructed from representative profiles [1]. Their inspected runner also clips thermal residence times to period length. Our raw-profile diagnostic retains the native 48-hour residence restriction. Accordingly, a raw-profile collision or a failure of an inherited commitment is not evidence that the authors' stated operating formulation fails.

Common commitment with adaptive dispatch is established robust unit commitment [2]. Minimax regret for energy time-series aggregation and bounds on objective loss are also explicit prior art [3]. Feasibility-driven representative-period refinement is established [4]. Independent exact certificate verification has a broader optimization precedent [5]. The present study tests a specific information/decision contract and the strength of its executable evidence; it does not introduce those general ideas.

## 2. Information, action and proof contracts

For a fixed input pair x0,x1, let O(x) be the declared preprocessing output for raw input x. Our stronger observation comparison includes Hindex, the complete 168-entry chronological period/within-day-index object, under the same compatible representative-period relabeling, within the declared projection containing 24 nodal-demand series, 11 utility-scale photovoltaic/wind capacity-factor series and 6 hydro-inflow series. The 17 named signal generators in the author metadata are only the latter two groups, not the total signal-table count. The original 107-coordinate raw sequence—41 generator lower bounds, 41 upper bounds, one aggregate balance input and 24 nodal-demand inputs per hour—is retained separately. The author revision is ce97428aa225037dcbcd848889ef55f3b67c17ba with TSAM 2.3.9, three representative periods of 24 hours, the declared aggregated no-storage tables, MinMaxScaler and Euclidean k-medoids. The declared execution environment is a version-pinned variant rather than the author’s entire original environment. The observation runs used Gurobi with a 30-second limit, one thread, seed 0 and MIPGap 0, requiring a returned numerical optimal status; exact clustering optimality and uniqueness are not claimed. Compatible representative-period label maps were checked explicitly. This observer contract does not cover every input or downstream module the author software could accept.

A deterministic diagnostic action chooses one complete 168-hour vector z=(U,Y,Z), where U is the on/off status, Y indicates a start and Z indicates a shutdown, from O before the raw member of the pair is revealed. Continuous dispatch and angles may adapt afterward. Static data, boundary conventions and action spaces agree. This entire-week two-stage contract is deliberately specified; it is not a rolling operational policy. The joint model shares 12,096 binary coordinates, has 33,936 columns and 69,362 original rows, and retains a nominal 23,195 MWh fossil-electricity cap for each world. The cap concerns electrical generation from 23 fossil units, not fuel cost or monetary welfare.

Exact arithmetic interprets every saved binary64 coefficient as its exact rational value. The checked model uses a uniform endpoint expansion tau=Fraction.from_float(1e-5), with exact binary states. This is an explicit mathematical convention spanning different row units. It is neither a calibrated physical uncertainty set nor a silent substitution for strict nominal feasibility. Positive and negative claims name that convention.

For i in {0,1}, let Fi be the set of commitments admitting adaptive dispatch in world i at the stated cap. A rejected fixed commitment establishes z not in Fi. Two successful individual witnesses establish F0 and F1 are nonempty. Opposite cross-transfer failures do not establish an empty intersection. A common verified witness establishes F0 intersect F1 is nonempty. A timeout, a numerical infeasibility status, or a rejected finite set of nominees establishes neither intersection result.

For a separate loss question, z ranges over the common original binary state domain with the stated boundaries. Let Ci(z) be minimum fossil-electricity generation in the expanded model without the cap, set Ci(z)=+infinity when recourse is infeasible, and let Ci* be its independently informed optimum. A positive minimax regret min_z max_i[Ci(z)-Ci*] would quantify an unavoidable loss for this two-input action contract. A difference between C1* and C0* does not prove that regret. This classical formulation is not newly proposed here [2,3]; no certified positive regret is currently claimed.

## 4. Operational evidence and exact state cuts

The first-week identity and repaired day321 worlds each have an independently admitted original-binary expanded witness. Their fossil-electricity totals are approximately 22,964.941240 and 23,024.941240 MWh. The repaired target increases that total by exactly 60 MWh through the specified local change; it is not a new optimality claim. The identity commitment fails on day321 through a one-hour aggregate capacity shortage. The repaired day321 commitment fails on identity through an already admitted necessary cut. These are two particular-policy results.

The unchanged joint continuous relaxation has an exactly verified feasible witness under the same tau expansion; strict nominal membership is not asserted. Thus a Farkas certificate of infeasibility for that unchanged relaxation cannot settle the missing binary question. The earlier generic mixed-integer search returned no admitted common witness; its timeout remains UNKNOWN. The later necessary master supplied one nominee, whose recourse attempt was numerically infeasible. A subsequent phase-I calculation provided an exact necessary state cut rejecting that nominee.

For completeness, let y collect continuous dispatch and angle variables. Write the original rows as l <= A_B z + A_C y <= u, where A_B and A_C are the state and continuous column blocks and l,u are the row endpoints. Let L_C <= y <= H_C be the finite continuous-variable boxes. Define A=[A_B A_C]. Choose any signed original-row vector d with finite selected endpoints and define q=A^T d and beta=sum_{d_r>0} d_r l_r + sum_{d_r<0} d_r u_r. Every feasible state in the expanded model satisfies

q_B z >= beta - support_[L_C,H_C](q_C) - tau (sum_r |d_r| + sum_j |q_C,j|).  (1)

Here q_B and q_C are the state and continuous parts of q, support_[L_C,H_C](q_C) is the maximum of q_C y over the named box, r indexes original rows and j indexes continuous columns. To obtain Eq. (1), sum the selected expanded row inequalities, then upper-bound q_C y by that support plus tau times its L1 norm. The state terms remain explicit. No extra binary-box or derived-row tolerance is deducted. This standard inequality does not require solver-optimal multipliers. The numerical phase-I output proposes d; the original matrix and exact arithmetic establish validity.

The admitted initial cut uses 24 nonzero original rows,21 state coefficients and 19 continuous residual coefficients, all at identity hour 80 (the 81st hour). Its support is aggregate balance,21 thermal upper rows, nodal balance at bus 107 and branch 10 (filtered index 10, upstream branch A11). There is no ramp, residence-time, transition or weekly-cap row in that support. The mechanism is a static network condition. Its positive weighted proof margin is approximately 5.300026405, not 5.3 MWh. The complete residual vector and support loss are retained; sparse support is not asserted to be minimum raw information or a minimum explanation.

The subsequent finite experiment applies this one adaptively chosen template to 2 × 168 world/hours before a maximum of eight new nominations and 24 optimizer calls. It uses the original model and cap, an explicitly outward numerical cut encoding, full backend readback, duplicate-state checks and independent final verification. It is one conventional refinement trajectory, not 336 independent findings or eight replications. Numerical failures, a missing certificate, an unsupported encoding or the declared ceiling stop the trajectory. There is no automatic extension or ninth nomination.

The sole attempt stopped before the first optimizer because a Windows process-ownership assignment failed. Both independent reviews closed all 336 original-matrix necessary cuts and requested outward numerical encodings. Every cut has 21 U coefficients and 19 continuous P residuals, with no Y/Z or angle residuals. No actual backend row, new nominee or adaptive cut exists. The producer phase took 97.2533605 seconds, with zero optimizer time; independent mathematical and encoding/lifecycle reviews took 10.1994726 and 4.8754401 seconds. This is an infrastructure failure and a verified seed family, not a completed eight-round search. Common-binary feasibility remains UNKNOWN. Any later infrastructure repair or scientific continuation is a separately declared arm and is not silently included in this result.

## 5. Separate actual-code chronology cost audit

A synthetic single-bus OR-LIB case was checked against actual UnitCommitment.jl 0.4.0 revision 4f04f0dd6641b071fd7556346c3d7190c2ffdfe5, including a target-specific native model export and full nominal encoding comparison. Exact lower/upper certificates were transferred with the required expanded-domain support correction; expanded-set equality is not asserted. The reverse-minus-identity optimal encoded-cost difference for the declared expanded native models has the outward interval[9972.898506,63039.576470]. Both endpoints derive from independently verified brackets, not two solver-reported incumbent objectives.

This is one post hoc native-code fidelity extension among the original two target orders times two service variants. The other three comparisons were not thereby validated against their actual native exports. The positive interval concerns separately informed objective optima with production, startup and penalized curtailment. It is not regret, fossil MWh, calibrated monetary impact, strict nominal optimality, a second transmission network or field validation. A strict nominal constructive attempt failed by an exact tiny bound violation; that failed attempt and the absent nominal upper are retained.

## 6. Reproducibility, limitations and publication scope

A standalone proof folder checks the initial necessary inequality and fixed-state rejection using Python's standard library only. A single clean copied-folder run on the same host reproduced the full residual/scalar claims in 2.4184489 seconds without an optimizer. This is a packaging test, not a second-machine replication or a new scientific result. The bundle does not rebuild physical network semantics, authenticate the model from external raw sources, or verify every extra diagnostic field in the producer file. Broader provenance and independent checks remain separately archived.

The executable observations, exact accepted claims, failed candidates, correction history and UNKNOWN results support a bounded reproducibility/evaluation study. They do not establish superiority over a matched operational baseline, priority of a general method, minimum information requirements or journal readiness. The strongest application mismatch is the raw-profile/native-dwell diagnostic versus the original authors' medoid-reconstructed reference. Same-system weeks are not independent-network transfer. The 45-work reading ledger distinguishes 39 selected/indexed-body readings and 6 limited records; it does not claim 45 complete readings or an exhaustive priority search.

Data and code availability: source, declared protocols, exact inputs, accepted evidence and preserved nulls are on the [experimental research branch](https://github.com/shaikhamalkawi-ux/Decision-Stability-Certificates-for-Power-System-Operating-States-/tree/codex/temporal-information).
