# Two direct precedents for certificate-guided refinement

27 September 2026. Bounded primary-source comparison; no optimizer, new experiment or full-text redistribution. Verdict: **generic verification-driven refinement and objective bounds are established. The current evidence does not establish a new refinement method.**

**Bahl, Söhler, Hennen and Bardow, “Typical Periods for Two-Stage Synthesis by Time-Series Aggregation with Bounded Error in Objective Function,” Frontiers in Energy Research 5:35 (published 8 January 2018), DOI [10.3389/fenrg.2017.00035](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/energy-research/articles/10.3389/fenrg.2017.00035/full).** Direct publisher body §§2.3–2.4 and §3.2.2 were re-read, extending/reconfirming the existing L04 record rather than adding another paper. The method fixes the aggregated design, checks full-series operation, and adds feasibility time steps when that operation fails. These additional steps have zero duration and no chronological links; peak steps alone can be insufficient. Its stated selection alternates maximum heating/cooling demand among infeasible steps. Further refinement increases representative periods or within-period segments using an objective-domain error criterion. Equation (19) compares aggregated cost with full-series operating cost for the fixed design; do not silently identify that particular expression with a globally valid lower/upper optimality bracket. This directly defeats novelty claims for “find a failing hour, retain it, verify again” or generic objective-guided temporal refinement.

**Bahl, Lützow, Shu, Hollermann, Lampe, Hennen and Bardow, “Rigorous synthesis of energy systems by decomposition via time-series aggregation,” Computers & Chemical Engineering 112:70–81 (2018), DOI [10.1016/j.compchemeng.2018.01.023](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0098135418300504).** Publisher metadata/abstract/introduction were inspected; the journal body remains unread. Body evidence instead comes from Bahl's [public RWTH dissertation](https://publications.rwth-aachen.de/record/730336/files/730336.pdf?subformat=pdfa), Chapter 6: printed p77 explicitly identifies the reused article; §§6.2–6.5, pp80–87, were directly read. They construct a relaxation lower bound by demand underestimation and relaxed balances, obtain an upper bound from full-series operation at fixed structure, and increase clustering resolution until the gap is acceptable. Page80 explicitly excludes chronological operating constraints from that decomposition. It is strong prior art for rigorous bound/refinement architecture, not an established equivalent of our chronological UC model. The chapter is a reading route for that work, not a second independent journal paper or an assertion of byte-identical versions.

The distinction potentially left to our work is narrower: an exact, archived certificate for a declared unchanged UC encoding can carry a verifiable rejection across a specified observation class; the closed fixed-ray stress arm also optimizes one such sufficient margin over a finite permutation family without repeated dispatch solves. That describes what was implemented. It supplies neither a new Farkas theorem nor a demonstrated superior refinement algorithm. A negative fixed-ray margin remains nonseparation; maximizing it does not maximize operating loss. The family-wide transfer nulls and the four feasible fixed-subset LPs must remain in the evidence. Previously inspected safe-bound and dual-proof-reuse literature additionally prevents treating exact arithmetic or proof reuse alone as novel.

The root's reported days321 support is especially damaging to a broad chronological-refinement claim: its proposed fixed-policy rejection uses one balance and hourly capacity rows, not a temporal constraint. Adding that offending hour would resemble the already established feasibility-step idea. Our three equal-observation-plus-Hindex cases instead expose within-cluster profile loss, the error source Auer explicitly separates from boundary handling. An independently checked fixed-policy contrast is useful evidence, but it is not yet a new chronological mechanism, unrestricted infeasibility or a demonstrated advantage over the two methods above. No matched operational comparator or refinement performance study has been executed.

**Decision:** position the present work as a precisely scoped, reproducible certificate/observation test until it demonstrates a benefit beyond conventional full-series verification and feasibility-step retention. A future methodological claim must specify its refinement rule and guarantee, keep the operational model fixed, and distinguish its added result from those existing baselines. This note does not authorize or prescribe another computation. The current exact artifacts remain valuable even if the generic novelty claim is rejected.
