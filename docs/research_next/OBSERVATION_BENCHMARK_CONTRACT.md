# Observation benchmark contract and complete case index

This is a navigation index of closed, independently checked preprocessing experiments. It introduces no new clustering, observation comparison, UC result or benchmark-performance measurement. Machine-readable [comparisons.csv](../../results/research_next/observation_case_index/comparisons.csv) contains all30 comparison records; [invocation_index.json](../../results/research_next/observation_case_index/invocation_index.json) contains all36 actual invocations. The source script verifies six pinned result/completion/review inputs, then copies fields into the index. It does not rerun their mathematical checks or solvers.

| Fixed experiment | Target comparisons | Additional controls | Actual clustering calls | Target primary equality | Target equality including full Hindex |
|---|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|
| HOD permutation challenge | 6 | 3positive controls and3identity repeats | 15 | 0/6 | Not separately evaluated in this stage |
| Whole-day permutation challenge | 15 | 3identity repeats | 21 | 5/15 | 3/15 |

Primary equality means equality of the complete declared supported-projection observation under a simultaneous representative-period label alignment: selected bus-demand, named VRES and hydro tables, both weight tables, circular transitions and both normalized matrices. The stronger whole-day comparison additionally requires the entire Hindex under the SAME compatible label map. Source-day identifiers are provenance. Exact stored table comparisons do not assert a unique clustering optimum or cross-platform determinism.

The actual pinned author preprocessing and official TSAM2.3.9 source are used with R=3 and24-hour representative periods in the explicitly recorded execution/environment variant. The support projection, no-storage contract and author input multiplicities remain those fixed in AUER_PROJECTION_PROTOCOL.md. This is not a reproduction of the author's operational UC implementation: the author's medoid-reconstructed reference and24-hour dwelling clip differ from the native48-hour operating model used in later decision tests. It is also not a comparison of optimization speed. Historical [HOD readout](../../results/research_next/auer_execution/READOUT.md) and [whole-day readout](../../results/research_next/whole_day_observation_preflight/READOUT.md) retain environment, warnings, limits and detailed proof bindings.

The families are sequential research stages. The whole-day family was chosen after the adverse HOD result; its15-case denominator was then fixed before execution. They are not interchangeable samples from a common random population. The three January weeks belong to the same RTS system; the transformed sequences are synthetic arrangements of recorded data. Controls/repeats are shown separately, not counted as favorable target findings or a false-positive rate. No confidence interval or generalized collision frequency follows from these chosen cases.

No UC model was solved in either observation stage. Later individual, fixed-schedule and common-decision results refer to separate artifacts and cannot be backdated into these records. In particular, the stronger week1/day321 collision later gained an individual expanded binary witness, while common-action feasibility was still UNKNOWN at the phase-I checkpoint. The CSV deliberately labels UC use only as false IN THE OBSERVATION STAGE, avoiding stale universal feasibility labels. A common schedule, exact universal exclusion or regret bound requires its own proof; observation equality alone supplies none.

The unrelated native UnitCommitment.jl OR-LIB cost-fidelity experiment is deliberately outside this observation index. Its independent synthetic one-bus data do not constitute another transmission-network test or an Auer-observer collision. Preserve its original four target/service comparisons and post-hoc native-fidelity scope in its own records.

To regenerate this index, use a separate scratch checkout or copy in which only this generated index directory is initially absent, preserving the released evidence. Run src/researchnext_observation_case_index.py with the standard-library Python runtime. It writes a new output directory once and fails if the existing closed index would be overwritten. The generated MANIFEST.json binds its inputs and outputs. Reproducing an index is not repeating the clustering or the scientific experiments, nor a second-machine replication.
