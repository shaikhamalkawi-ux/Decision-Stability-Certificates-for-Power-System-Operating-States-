# Conditional route from a permutation family to an observer-invariant family

27 September 2026. Source/theory proposal only, written after the closed whole-day observation experiment and before any new clustering calculation. No scientific computation is authorized by this note. The current HOD fixed-ray extremum is not an extremum over the authentic observer's fiber.

## Question and elementary conditional statement

Let a raw day consist of all jointly retained physical coordinates at its24 ordered hours. Suppose a preprocessing map forms day features without using absolute day position, commutes with day permutation, and chooses a unique clustering partition and unique final within-cluster representative profiles, up to cluster relabeling. Here uniqueness refers to the actual mathematical objective and all subsequent representative-selection steps, not a numerical solver's Optimal flag. Suppose cluster assignment of every raw day is unique as well. Fixed static metadata and within-day positions are unchanged.

Consider permutations that only exchange complete raw days assigned to the same original cluster, with all protected edge days fixed. Under those hypotheses, every destination receives the same cluster label as before; the cluster membership multisets, selected representative profiles and weights are unchanged. The full positioned Hindex is then unchanged up to the same label map. Consequently its circular transition counts and normalized transition matrices are unchanged. The complete configured preprocessing observation used in the closed study is equal across this constructed family.

This is an elementary conditional equivariance argument, not a new clustering theorem, proof about the actual saved computation, or characterization of the entire observation fiber. The constructed family is only a subset of that fiber if every stated hypothesis is established. Non-unique optima, first-index tie rules, approximate optimization, preprocessing arithmetic that depends on row order, or additional consumed positional data can invalidate the inference. Selected medoid source indices are provenance; equality must concern actual consumed profiles.

## Why this might give a useful bounded next step

On the saved seven-day, three-representative-day problem, there are only35 possible sets of three candidate centers before assignment. A separate exact finite audit of the **saved distance coefficients and declared center/assignment problem** might settle uniqueness or exhibit ties without another clustering solve. This would not automatically establish uniqueness of TSAM's subsequent within-cluster medoid recomputation, equivalence with unrounded Euclidean arithmetic, or permutation equivariance of every floating-point preprocessing step. Those are separate obligations. If the uniqueness condition fails, record that failure rather than choose favorable tie rules.

The closed15-target experiment already computed every interior-day order under the first/last48-hour restriction. For the same three interior days, discovering an invariant two-point family would supply interpretation, not a new empirical extremizer or expanded operating denominator. Its possible value is a rigorously delimited construction for a larger future case, only if the stated conditions can be checked without effectively rerunning every chronology. No larger case is selected here.

## Conditional certificate optimization

Suppose additionally that the UC matrix and coordinate system are invariant over the constructed family, and every changing row/column endpoint belongs to one complete destination-day package. For one fixed signed ray, its exact expanded separation margin then has the form

`G(pi) = constant + sum_destination_day cost[destination_day, assigned_source_day]`.

Within each exchangeable cluster, the source-day permutation must be bijective. Maximizing this one margin is a linear assignment problem per cluster, with protected positions removed. It is not24 independent hour choices; whole-day coherence and cluster membership couple those choices. The finite costs can be treated as exact rationals. This is a classical assignment decomposition under stated separability assumptions, not an unproved claim of a new optimization algorithm.

A positive maximum provides one certificate-backed negative member of the declared family. A nonpositive maximum means that this one ray separates none of that family; it does not establish feasibility. A positive minimum would reject every member for the same fixed mathematical predicate. No such result is computed by this note. If the family includes an independently verified feasible anchor for that predicate, a sound positive minimum is impossible and would indicate a binding/model/checker error.

For the old fixed-identity commitment, any statement is about recourse conditional on that commitment. For an unrestricted full-model ray, it concerns the corresponding unrestricted relaxation. A method that produces a different commitment requires its own check. Maximizing a sufficient certificate margin is not maximizing the true operating cost or a complete robust-feasibility oracle.
