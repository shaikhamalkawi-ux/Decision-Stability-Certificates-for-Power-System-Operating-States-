Implementation requirements and cautions:

- The old `researchnext_orlib_uc.py` point checkers are not Fraction-candidate APIs: `finite` permits only int/float and `frac` reconstructs a binary64 rational. Do not pass the new rational point through float to reuse those checkers. New Fraction-aware point/semantic evaluators must preserve numerator/denominator values throughout; the existing native coefficient decoder may be reused explicitly.
- `limit_denominator(1000000)` means a fixed maximum denominator, not a common 1,000,000-point grid. It has no guaranteed reconstruction radius here. Do it once; a subsequent headroom, ramp, balance, box or equality failure must remain a failed construction rather than trigger clipping or a new Q choice.
- Use actual archived segment allowances, balance coefficients, initial-ramp endpoints and all other nominal rows exactly. Do not recompute a binary64 endpoint such as the initial-ramp RHS by a different real-arithmetic expression. The sum of separately rounded segment widths need not be assumed identical to a separately rounded headroom width; both restrictions must pass.
- The C construction may alter load curtailment and encoded cost. A positive is a native-penalized-model upper; it does not establish zero curtailment or hard-service feasibility. Report the new C values and actual objective rather than describing it as unchanged dispatch. The selected input has zero reserve requirements by its pinned parser contract, so this case cannot validate nonzero-reserve performance generally.
- Specify the 512-bit guard precisely for numerators, denominators and relevant intermediate accumulations. Small individual denominators do not guarantee that their summed objective has a small denominator. A bit/time stop is an incomplete heuristic attempt, never nominal infeasibility. If construction fails before all coordinates exist, retain the partial rational ledger and reason; do not fabricate a full point merely to satisfy the proposed full-candidate output rule.
