Substituting full-week commitment for equipment design changes the application and temporal constraints, not the regret theory. Robust common commitment/adaptive dispatch and Benders decomposition are also established; Bahl's feasibility-step and bound-driven refinement already cover generic “find a failing period, retain it, verify again.” Exact rational certificates strengthen evidence, not methodological priority. [Regret assessment](REGRET_PRIOR_ART_2023.md), [refinement assessment](REFINEMENT_PRIOR_ART_LIMITS.md), [decision criterion](POST_COLLISION_DECISION_CRITERION.md).

The useful remaining distinction is therefore an **exact, nonzero unavoidable loss for a real complete-observer collision under a declared action/information contract**, or a verified small-loss common action that refutes that hypothesis for the tested pair. The two individual optimal costs being different is not that distinction. Nor would another rejected fixed nominee settle it. No unresolved prior-art premise currently justifies another literature search before this decision.
