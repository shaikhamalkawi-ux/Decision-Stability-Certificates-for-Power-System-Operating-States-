*Simplifying capacity planning for electricity systems with hydroelectric and renewable generation*, Computational Management Science20,26. [Publisher](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10287-023-00451-5), DOI10.1007/s10287-023-00451-5. The paper distinguishes that economic regret from comparing reduced/full objective values directly. Its representative days retain prototype chronology and use alternative features/scalings; the hydro case highlights seasonal state differences despite similar profiles. This further rules out novelty claims for full-model decision replay, regret evaluation, or the general observation that similar profiles can have different intertemporal consequences. It does not establish our exact observation fiber or binary common-action result.
